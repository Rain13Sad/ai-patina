Three months of full remote, then we pulled the plug.

Not because of output. Commits went up 12%. It was the two new grads. They spent three months asking questions in the team channel and never once asked the right person, because they had no idea who knew what. Remote deletes the sentence "while I've got you" — and that sentence is how juniors actually learn.

I don't buy the productivity studies. They measure teams that already know each other.

Here's the question nobody asks: how much of what your company knows travels through hallways? Nobody has that number. It decides whether you can go remote at all.
